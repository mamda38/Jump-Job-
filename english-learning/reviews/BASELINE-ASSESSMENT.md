## Hướng dẫn

1. Bấm giờ và đọc một lượt không dùng từ điển.
2. Ghi thời gian đọc và mức hiểu tự đánh giá từ 0–100%.

5. Sau đó có thể tra từ và ghi số lần tra cứu cần thiết để hiểu rõ bài.

### Why some cities still protect cash payments

Digital payments have become a normal part of daily life. Many people use a phone or a bank card to pay for transport, meals, and even small purchases. For businesses, digital systems can make transactions faster and reduce the need to store cash. Customers may also find it easier to review their spending because every payment appears in an app or bank account.

However, a completely cashless economy can create difficulties. Older adults may be less comfortable with new technology, while people without a bank account cannot always use digital services. Technical problems are another concern. If a payment network fails or a phone loses power, a person may suddenly be unable to buy something essential.

For these reasons, some city governments have introduced rules requiring shops to accept cash. Supporters say such rules prevent vulnerable groups from being excluded. They view cash as a public option that should remain available even when most customers prefer electronic payments.

Critics argue that businesses should choose which payments they accept. Handling cash takes time, creates security risks, and can increase operating costs. They believe competition will encourage shops to offer the methods their customers actually want.

The debate is therefore not simply about old and new technology. It concerns convenience, fairness, business freedom, and society's ability to continue functioning when digital systems are unavailable.
