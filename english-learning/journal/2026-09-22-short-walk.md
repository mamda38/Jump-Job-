# Nhật ký: Making time for a short walk

## Yêu cầu

## Bài viết đầu tiên

I want to establish a regular exercise routine-perhaps jogging in the late afternoon or evening after work, since i live near a lake with an open. Although i have always wanted to do this, laziness holds me back. I hope to overcome this and successfully maintain the habit.
