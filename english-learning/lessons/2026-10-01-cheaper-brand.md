Ôn hai cụm in advance và keep the same price trong 2 phút.

Do you usually buy the same brand of food? Trả lời một câu ngắn.

Mục tiêu: đọc lấy ý, rồi trả lời đủ các vế. Chỉ đọc đoạn 128 từ trên giao diện; chưa mở nguồn, gợi ý hoặc từ vựng.

## 3. Đọc

### Trying a cheaper brand

When food prices rise, shoppers may change what they buy. An Associated Press report described people choosing cheaper brands, visiting discount stores, or buying fewer snacks. These choices can help households control their spending.

Consider this imaginary example. Mai buys cereal every week. Her usual box costs four dollars. A different brand costs three dollars for the same weight. She checks the ingredients and buys one box to try.

Mai likes the cheaper cereal, so she buys it again. She saves one dollar each week without buying less food. However, she keeps her usual tea because she prefers its taste.

Her choices show that saving money does not require changing everything. Comparing similar products and trying a small amount can help a shopper decide what works for them.

## 4. Kiểm tra hiểu — 5 phút

1. What is the main purpose of the passage?
2. What two things does Mai check before trying the different cereal brand?
3. How much does Mai save each week, and why does she keep her usual tea?
4. Why might buying only one box be a useful first step when trying a different brand?

Trả lời 1–2 câu ngắn mỗi câu hỏi. Với câu có hai phần, kiểm tra đã trả lời cả hai; không cần dịch toàn bài.

## 6. Cấu trúc — 2 phút

### without + V-ing

Tìm câu chứa without trong đoạn 3 và xác định hành động không xảy ra.

Ví dụ mới: I relax without watching videos.

Viết một câu ngắn theo khung: I can ___ without ___.

1. What food do you buy every week?

3. Would you try a different brand? Why?
