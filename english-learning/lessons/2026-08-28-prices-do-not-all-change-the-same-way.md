### Prices do not all change in the same way

Prices in Germany were higher in January 2026 than one year earlier. The inflation rate was 2.1%. Inflation means that the general cost of goods and services rises over time. However, not every price moved in the same direction.

Food became more expensive. Prices rose for fruit, meat, sweets, and chocolate. Services also cost more, especially transport, rent, and some social services. These increases made daily life more expensive for many consumers.

Energy was different. Household electricity and natural gas became cheaper than a year earlier. Lower energy prices helped balance some of the increases in food and services. Butter, cooking oils, and potatoes also became cheaper.

This shows why one inflation number cannot describe every product. A rate of 2.1% does not mean all prices rose by exactly 2.1%. Some prices rose faster, while others fell. Consumers experience inflation differently because they buy different things.

## 6. Cấu trúc

### become/became + tính từ

Dùng để mô tả một sự thay đổi trạng thái. *Became* là dạng quá khứ của *become*.

> Food became more expensive.
>
> Electricity became cheaper.

Hãy tạo một câu về một mặt hàng gần đây.

### Some..., while others...

Dùng để đối chiếu hai nhóm.

> Some prices rose, while others fell.

Hãy tạo một câu so sánh hai loại chi phí.
