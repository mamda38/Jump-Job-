### Making time for a short walk

Walking can be a simple way to add movement to daily life. An Associated Press report explains that people do not need a gym membership to start. Walking with friends can also help people stay motivated. Short walks can be spread across the day instead of taking place in one long session.

Here is an imaginary example. Lan works in an office and has a busy schedule. She wants to walk for thirty minutes, but she rarely has a long break. She decides to take three short walks: one before work, one after lunch, and one in the evening. Each walk lasts ten minutes.

Her colleague Minh prefers one long walk after work. Sometimes a late meeting changes his plans, and he misses the whole walk. Lan may also miss a walk, but she can still do the other two.

These plans show two ways to use the same amount of time. A useful plan should fit a person's daily routine.
