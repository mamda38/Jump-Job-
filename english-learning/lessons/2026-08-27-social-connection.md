## 2. Trước khi đọc

Dựa vào tiêu đề, bạn nghĩ những “small actions” nào có thể giúp mọi người kết nối với nhau?

## 3. Đọc

Đọc lượt đầu không dùng từ điển và bấm giờ riêng cho phần đọc. Sau đó viết một câu nêu ý chính bằng lời của bạn.

### Small actions can rebuild social connection

Many people in the United States are spending less time with others. They join fewer clubs and community groups than people did in the past. Some also have fewer close friends. This social isolation can make people feel lonely and may affect their health.

Small local groups are trying to rebuild connection. In Baltimore, neighbors created shared places where people can meet, exchange food, and help care for children. In Ohio, community organizations support affordable housing, small businesses, and public spaces such as parks and coffee shops.

These projects do not offer one large solution. Instead, they create regular chances for people to talk and work together. Supporters believe personal relationships can make a neighborhood stronger. When people know one another, they may be more willing to give practical help during difficult times.

The work is often slow because trust takes time to grow. However, local leaders say small actions can still matter. A shared meal, a safe place to meet, or a simple conversation may reduce isolation. Their main idea is that communities become healthier when people build connections step by step.

## 6. Cấu trúc

### can/may + động từ nguyên mẫu

Dùng *can* cho khả năng chung và *may* cho một khả năng có thể xảy ra.

> Social isolation can affect health.
>
> Neighbors may offer practical help.

Hãy tạo một câu với *can* hoặc *may* về cộng đồng nơi bạn sống.

### When + hiện tại đơn, can/may + động từ

> When people know one another, they may help more often.

Hãy tạo một câu bắt đầu bằng *When people...*
